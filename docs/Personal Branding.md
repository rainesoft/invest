This document outlines how to carefully separate your corporate identity (LinkedIn) from your founder/builder identity (X), allowing you to aggressively grow the Rainebank brand without compromising your current position as a banking CIO.

## 1. The Legal Question: Is this legal?

Yes, this dual-identity approach is very common in the tech industry. However, you must navigate one critical legal minefield: **The word "Bank".**

In almost every country (especially the US, UK, and EU), it is strictly illegal to market yourself as a "Bank" or offer "Banking Services" (like holding deposits) unless you have a chartered banking license. 

**How to stay legal while building Rainebank:**
- **Do NOT** say "I am building a bank" on X.
- **DO** say "I am building the future of automated wealth" or "I am building an AI-driven financial platform."
- **Do NOT** allow users to deposit money directly into Rainebank to hold as a balance.
- **DO** continue what we are doing: Rainebank acts as the "software brain" that connects to their *existing* regulated broker (Exness) via API. Exness holds the money, Rainebank just sends the math. This legally makes Rainebank a **SaaS (Software as a Service) / Fintech company**, not a bank!

Once you scale to millions in revenue, you can apply for a banking charter or acquire a small regional bank to officially accept deposits.
