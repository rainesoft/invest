# System Health Check Skill

This skill allows the agent to autonomously verify the execution integrity, database background jobs, and risk guardrails of the Raine Bank autonomous trading system.

## Usage
Whenever the user requests a "system health check", you should:

1. Refer to the diagnostic protocols found in `docs/System Health Checklist.md`.
2. Run the unified diagnostics query:
   ```bash
   npx supabase db query --linked --file scripts/full_health_audit.sql
   ```
   Or execute `python3 scripts/comprehensive_healthcheck.py` to fetch real-time diagnostics via the REST API.
3. Verify the output to ensure:
   - `pg_cron` jobs (all 15 active canonical jobs) are firing successfully with `status = 'succeeded'`, have zero duplicate/overlapping jobs (Section 1H), have no missing `timeout_milliseconds` or `x-cron-secret` configurations (proactive sweep in Section 1B), and enforce 24/7 weekend execution for `position-manager-poll` (`*/5 * * * *`) and `agent-news-poll` (`0 * * * *`) per Section 1P.
   - No silent agent crashes are occurring (query `audit_log` for `AGENT_CRASH`).
   - Autonomous agents (`agent-news`, `agent-day`, `agent-swing` split crons) are generating signals and heartbeats.
   - Database Webhook & Cron HTTP responses in `net._http_response` (queried via `public.check_http_response_errors()` RPC) have zero network failures (`Couldn't resolve host name`), no unhandled 500 server errors, no silent `System Error` / `ReferenceError` / `TypeError` strings in response bodies (Section 1N), and decommissioned functions do not flood logs with 401s from external legacy pings (Section 1L).
    - PostgREST queries avoid unconstrained embedded table joins (Section 1J), queries targeting `trade_opportunities` by agent name filter by `source` rather than `model_id` UUID (Section 2E), signal inserts strictly normalize `side` to `'LONG' | 'SHORT'` (handling `REQUIRE_LTF_DRILLDOWN` via `trade_watchlists` per Section 2F), Central Bank currency interventions trigger pre-AI vetoes without opposing sovereign flow (Section 2K), MetaAPI market data fallback maps candidate broker symbol aliases (e.g. `NAS100` -> `USTEC`, `GER30` -> `DE30`, `SPX500` -> `US500` per Section 2G), and cross-asset contract sizing ensures proper quote currency divisor conversions (e.g., JPY division for `JP225` per Section 2L, `assetLotCaps` for `ETHUSD: 0.04`, `BTCUSD: 0.02`, US stocks `AAPL`/`TSLA`/`NVDA`/`AMZN`/`MSFT`/`META`/`GOOGL: 0.02` per Section 2P).
    - Orphaned signals (stuck in `PUBLISHED`, `APPROVED` without `user_trades`, or `PENDING`) are zero, and Execution Desk volume skips explicitly mark `status = 'REJECTED'` (Section 3I) while index CFD micro-lots enforce `0.01` minimum volume (Section 3O).
    - Inter-agent HTTP calls construct sibling URLs using `SUPABASE_URL/functions/v1` and avoid referencing undefined `WEBHOOK_URL` (Section 4B), and Telegram broadcast webhooks properly escape all MarkdownV2 reserved characters (`\(`, `\)`, `\.`, `\-`) with zero parse entity errors and track broadcast failures via `TELEGRAM_BROADCAST_FAILURE` audit logs (Section 4A, 4C).
    - Closed trades with calculated `profit_usd` are not desynced in `status = 'OPEN'` (reconcile to `WON` or `LOST` via Section 3G, Step 1c).
    - Stale unfilled pending orders older than their 20-bar horizon (10h for 30m intraday, 20 days for 1D swing) are zero or garbage-collected to `status = 'CLOSED'` with parent opportunities marked `EXPIRED`, while active live `OPEN` broker trades are protected from premature cancellation and have `open_price` synchronized from EA callbacks (Section 3Q).
    - Stop loss evaluations adhere to the Bar-Close Invalidation rule (governed on confirmed candle close, allowing intra-bar liquidity wicks to breathe unless the $2.0\times$ ATR catastrophic emergency stop is reached).
    - Database triggers (e.g. `allocate_virtual_pnl()`) are idempotent and skip duplicate reference codes without throwing `23505 duplicate key` errors.
    - Broker execution errors in `user_trades` (MT5 error codes 10013 Invalid Request / Symbol Alias, 10014 Invalid Volume, 10015 Invalid Price, 10016 Invalid Stops / Multi-Leg TP Direction, 10018 Market Closed, 10019 Margin, 10044 Close Only, `MARGIN_LEVEL_BELOW_300`, `INSUFFICIENT_FREE_MARGIN_BUFFER` per Section 3V) are caught and parent opportunities updated to `REJECTED`, enforcing broker symbol alias resolution (`SPX500` -> `US500`, `NAS100` -> `USTEC`, `GER30` -> `DE30`, US stocks -> `NVDA_m`/`NVDAm`/`NVDA.pro` per Section 3K & Section 3P), validating `SYMBOL_TRADE_MODE_FULL`, and enforcing the Pre-Flight Market Hours Execution Guard (Section 1Q) with Friday 21:00 UTC closure across traditional assets in `agent-trade` and `agent-day` to eliminate Code:10018 on weekend webhook handoffs.
    - Active Position Exposure & Stacking Concurrency Guard (Section 2N) is enforced in `agent-swing` (Layer -0.5), `agent-day`, and `agent-trade`, blocking duplicate concurrent open opportunities on the same symbol or correlated equity basket (`AAPL`, `TSLA`, `NVDA`, `AMZN`, `MSFT`, `META`, `GOOGL` per Section 2P) to prevent free margin exhaustion (`Code:10019` / `MARGIN_LEVEL_BELOW_300`).
    - Adaptive Limit Inversion Guard (Section 1R & Section 3S) in `agent-trade` and `vps-poll` automatically converts crossed pending limit orders (`BUY LIMIT` $\ge$ market ask or `SELL LIMIT` $\le$ market bid) to market orders, fetching live candle prices via `fetchRecentBars` without throwing `ReferenceError: currentPrice is not defined` or MT5 `Code:10016` / `Code:10044` rejections. All Edge Functions strictly pass Deno TypeScript type checking, correctly index into typed `LogicContext` array fields (`htf_support?.[0]`, `htf_resistance?.[0]`), and pre-flight spread guard rejections (`SPREAD_TOO_WIDE`) are cleanly parsed in SRE telemetry (Section 3T).
    - Institutional algorithmic execution filters (Section 3U) are enforced: Auction Market Theory Value Area pre-filter discards mid-range chop (30%-70% VA) in `agent-day`, Late-Week Swing Entry Gate freezes non-crypto 1D origination on late Thursday (>=16:00 UTC) and Friday in `agent-swing`, Floor Trader Pivot Alignment Guard prevents blind sub-pivot long and super-pivot short traps in `agent-day`, Wilder's ADX momentum scaling expands TP1 targets when $ADX \ge 30$, Pre-AI Cross-Asset Correlation Lock prevents contradictory exposure across indices/metals/energy/FX/equities, Dynamic Limit Fill Urgency factor ($0.04\times\text{ ATR}$) eliminates missed momentum fills, Rollover Spread Gate halts non-crypto origination during 21:00-22:15 UTC spread spikes, Hive Mind 1D Macro Directives enforce top-down multi-timeframe directional hierarchy, Late-Session Liquidity Cutoff halts non-crypto intraday entries between 18:30-22:00 UTC, Dual Indicator Divergence Conflict Veto blocks counter-trend momentum exhaustion entries in `agent-day` & `agent-swing`, NY Open Opening Cross Protection (13:30-14:45 UTC) converts market orders to structural limit pullbacks ($0.04\times\text{ ATR}$), Pre-AI Asian Session FX Routing bypasses non-Asian pairs between 22:00-06:00 UTC to eliminate redundant LLM token spend, Relative Volume (RVOL >= 0.65x) & Structural Geometry Hurdle gates discard low-volume and compressed-headroom setups pre-AI, Institutional Session Open Liquidity Multiplier grants a +5 confidence bonus during London (06:30-09:30 UTC) and NY (12:30-15:30 UTC) opens, Early Breakeven Protection locks Stop Loss at $+0.05\text{R}$ once price reaches $+0.50\text{R}$ or touches Target 1, Strict Target 1 Floor Normalization enforces $|TP1 - \text{Entry}| \ge \max(0.80\text{R}, 0.80\times\text{ ATR})$ in `agent-swing`, Swing Index CFD Market-to-Limit Converter forces $0.04\times\text{ ATR}$ pullback limit orders during $ADX < 30$, and Nikkei 225 (`JP225`) USDJPY & Yen Macro Sentiment Inheritance penalizes Nikkei longs when USDJPY is bearish.
    - All TP targets (`tp1`, `tp2`, `tp3`) enforce the 3-Layer Direction Validation against entry prices across `agent-swing`, `agent-trade`, and `vps-poll`, verifying Target 2 satisfies $\ge 1:1.70$ R:R.
    - Runner legs enforce the Chandelier ATR dynamic trail ($2.0\times\text{ ATR}$) and stepped profit floors ($+0.75\text{R}$ at $+1.5\text{R}$, $+1.25\text{R}$ at $+2.0\text{R}$, $+2.0\text{R}$ at $+3.0\text{R}$) per Section 3M.
    - Edge Functions strictly employ `.maybeSingle()` and avoid `.single()` on user/trade/opportunity lookups to eliminate unhandled `PGRST116 (0 rows)` crashes across background crons (Section 3N).
    - `vps-history` callback adheres to canonical schema (`profit_usd`, `error_message`, `status = WON | LOST`), dynamically updates user capital and high-water mark, and skips unmapped manual/external MT5 tickets with `status = 'EXTERNAL_SKIPPED'` rather than 404 (Section 3J).
    - `vps-callback` uses `.maybeSingle()` and returns HTTP 200 `OK` on unmapped/external callback tickets rather than throwing 500 errors (Section 3L), and captures exact fill price (`&price=`) to populate `open_price` (Section 3Q).
    - Stale `ACTIVE` trade opportunities older than 24 hours without live `OPEN` positions are safely expired to `status = 'EXPIRED'` (complying with `trade_opportunities_status_check` constraint), while live `OPEN` broker positions are protected from premature expiration in `resolve-outcomes` and auto-healed to `ACTIVE` by `agent-sre` Probe 4H (Section 3R).
    - Completed trades (`WON`/`LOST`/`CLOSED`) have their parent `trade_opportunities` reconciled immediately (`WON` for positive net PnL, `LOST` for negative net PnL, `EXPIRED` for cancelled/missed entries) without lingering in `ACTIVE`.
    - `vps-poll` receives fresh MT5 heartbeats (within 60s) dynamically queried across master accounts, formats stock CFDs to 2 decimal places and `JP225` to 1 decimal place, and streams live 30m candles (`market_data_pti`).
    - Hourly autonomous SRE watchdog `agent-sre` executed via `agent-sre-poll` audits all 10 subsystem probes (including cron failures, HTTP errors via RPC, agent crashes & critical errors, pipeline self-healing, symbol exposure stacking Probe 4I, VPS heartbeats, market data freshness, treasury solvency, AI model timeout sweeps, and drawdown/circuit-breaker limits), auto-heals status desyncs, and records `SRE_HEARTBEAT`.
    - AI model evaluation timeouts (`API_TIMEOUT` in `audit_log`) are zero or transient, backed by exponential retry resiliency and token truncation quote/brace repair in `agent-day`, `agent-swing`, and `agent-news` (Section 2O).
    - Account drawdown limits (5% daily, 10% total max drawdown) are unbreached across all active user PAMM accounts.
4. If anomalies or status desyncs are detected, apply the reconciliation SQL from Section 2N, Section 2O, Section 2P, Section 3G, Section 3H, Section 3I, Section 3P, Section 3Q, Section 3R, Section 3S, Section 3T, Section 3U, and Section 3V of `docs/System Health Checklist.md` and report findings to the user.
